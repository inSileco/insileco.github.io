This webinar introduces practical, research-ready data management for academics working in interdisciplinary programs, from planning and documentation to storage, archiving, sharing, and ethical governance. It highlights actionable approaches to Data Management Plans (DMPs), FAIR/CARE/TRUST principles, metadata standards, and interoperable workflows that support collaboration and reuse across teams. The webinar was developed and delivered by inSileco in collaboration with ArcticNet as part of ongoing efforts to strengthen research data practices and long-term stewardship.
