## Intro

We work at the intersection of environmental science, data systems, and decision-making, in collaboration with public agencies, Indigenous organizations, and research partners.

## What we look for

We’re always happy to hear from people with strengths in:

- Environmental data science and analytics
- Software engineering and data pipelines
- Ecological modeling and decision support
- Training, workshops, and capacity building
- Technical writing and knowledge sharing
